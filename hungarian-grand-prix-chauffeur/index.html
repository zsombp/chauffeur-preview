Hungarian Grand Prix 2027: Race Day Chauffeur Package

30 July to 1 August 2027 · Hungaroring, Mogyoród · Door to door from your Budapest hotel

No surpriseFixed priceTax & tolls included

Spend the race weekend at the track, not in the traffic. Your private Mercedes and English-speaking chauffeur collect you at your hotel, drive you to the circuit entrance agreed with you, stay in the circuit area for the whole day and take you back after the chequered flag. One fixed price per car, confirmed in writing before you travel.

Message us on WhatsApp

Reserve early. Race-weekend bookings are accepted at least 14 days in advance and are confirmed with a 50% deposit. On race days, Friday to Sunday, we run full-day hires only, and race-day cars are allocated in order of booking: the weekend sells out early.

Your Race Day: how it works

  1. Pick-up at your hotel

    On race days we leave early: your chauffeur agrees the departure time with you the day before, with a traffic buffer.

  2. Drive to the Hungaroring

    The circuit is about 20 km north-east of the city centre; we drop you at the entrance agreed with you. Where the organiser requires parking or access permits for the zones closest to the gates, those are the organiser's: we plan the drop-off around the traffic rules of the day.

  3. Your car waits for you

    Chauffeur and car stay in the circuit area all day (the waiting time is part of the package) and come to the meeting point agreed with you when you call. You leave when you decide, not when the shuttle buses do.

  4. Back to the city

    Return to your hotel, or to the restaurant of your choice, within the package hours.

What's included

  • Mercedes-Benz E-Class, V-Class or S-Class (VIP V-Class and G-Class on request)
  • Professional, English-speaking chauffeur
  • All waiting time within the package hours
  • Fuel, motorway tolls and VAT
  • Bottled water
  • Free Wi-Fi

Not included

  • Race tickets (we are a chauffeur company, not a ticket seller)
  • Circuit parking or access permits, if the organiser requires them
  • Tour guide (available on request, at extra cost)

Good to know

Will there be traffic?

Yes: tens of thousands of spectators converge on the circuit, and a drive that takes half an hour on a normal day can take several hours on race days. Your chauffeur plans the departure with a buffer and knows the approach roads.

Where does the car wait during the race?

In the public parking area nearest to your entrance, or wherever the organiser's traffic plan allows on the day; your chauffeur agrees the meeting point with you when you get out and comes when you call.

How many passengers?

E-Class and S-Class: up to 3 passengers; V-Class: up to 5 passengers with luggage.

Can we stop on the way back (dinner, a bar)?

Yes, within the package hours; additional hours at the race-weekend hourly rate.

Do you sell tickets or hospitality packages?

No. We provide chauffeured transport only; tickets are sold by the official organiser.

Can you also pick us up at the airport?

On Thursday 29 July and Monday 2 August, at the race-weekend rate. From Friday to Sunday we run full-day hires exclusively: an airport pick-up on those days is possible as part of a Race Day package.

Arriving via Vienna or Bratislava?

Our city-to-city transfers are available on Thursday and Monday at the published city-to-city rates; from Friday to Sunday only as part of a Race Day package. Ask for a quote.
