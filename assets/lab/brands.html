Seven names, seven identities, one chauffeur service

Proposals to react to, not decisions. Each one has a name, the idea behind it, a symbol, a wordmark, colours, type, and a slice of the real website dressed in it. The car is our own render, lit differently for each identity.

Five are mine, two (Quiet Mile and Budapest Chauffeur Co.) are Codex's, and Codex scored all of them as a second opinion: its notes are blunt on purpose.

Nothing here is registered or checked for trademarks. Domain notes are a DNS lookup on 2026-09-21, which only shows whether something answers today.

How to read this: pick by the name first (say it on the phone, read it on a name sign), then by the mood. Colours and type can move between names; a name cannot be fixed later.

Before any of these is used: a trademark search in the EU and Hungary (classes 39 and 12), the .com and .hu domains, the Instagram and Google Business Profile handles, and a native English speaker saying it out loud.
