Parndorf Designer Outlet: a full shopping day from Budapest, door to door

About 2 to 2.5 hours' drive · Mercedes E-, V- or S-Class with professional chauffeur · your car waits for you · optional Vienna: coffee, schnitzel, photo stops, or the luxury houses

No surpriseFixed priceTax & tolls included

One of Central Europe's largest designer outlets, Designer Outlet Parndorf, with more than 150 brand stores, is barely two and a half hours from Budapest, just across the Austrian border. Your chauffeur collects you at your hotel, drops you at the outlet entrance and waits there all day: the bags go in the boot, not in your hands. On the way back, if you like, Vienna: coffee and cake in a classic coffee house, or a real Wiener Schnitzel, with a few photo stops. And for the big fashion houses not represented in Budapest, Vienna's Kohlmarkt and Graben are under an hour from the outlet, or a day trip of their own.

Three packages

  1. A) Outlet day (about 10 hours)

    1. 08:00 pick-up at your hotel
    2. about 10:30 arrival at the outlet
    3. 5 hours of shopping while car and chauffeur wait on site
    4. 15:30 departure

  2. B) Outlet + Vienna (about 12 hours, 08:00 to 20:00)

    1. 08:00 pick-up

    3. 14:30 drive to Vienna (about 45 minutes)
    4. 15:15 to 17:30 Vienna: a stroll along Kärntner Straße, coffee and cake in a classic coffee house or a schnitzel at one of the city's famous schnitzel houses, photo stops at the Opera, St Stephen's Cathedral and the Ringstraße
    5. 17:30 departure
    6. about 20:00 back in Budapest

  3. C) Vienna: the luxury houses (about 12 hours, 08:00 to 20:00)

    1. 08:00 pick-up at your hotel
    2. about 10:45 Vienna's first district: Kohlmarkt, Graben and Tuchlauben, where the big fashion and jewellery houses sit side by side, among them names not represented in Budapest, such as Bulgari, Van Cleef & Arpels, Dior, Cartier and Hermès, and the official Patek Philippe retailers
    3. your car waits nearby and comes when you call; the bags go in the boot, not in your hands
    4. coffee and cake or lunch in a classic Viennese house
    5. about 20:00 back in Budapest

    Or combine the two: the outlet in the morning, the Vienna boutiques in the afternoon.

The timings are a sketch. You shape the day: outlet only, more time in Vienna, or lunch in Vienna instead of coffee.

Good to know

  • Opening days. The outlet and the Vienna shops are open Monday to Saturday; on Sundays and Austrian public holidays the shops are closed: the trip is bookable Monday to Saturday. A few of the Vienna houses keep their own hours, and some are closed on Mondays as well: tell us which ones you want to visit and we check them for your date.
  • Border. Hungary and Austria are both in the Schengen area: no border queue; please carry your passport or ID card anyway, spot checks do happen.
  • Tax-free shopping. Visitors resident outside the EU can reclaim VAT under the Austrian tax-free scheme; the conditions and paperwork come from the shops: we are not involved in the refund.
  • Boot space. The E-Class boot takes a couple's shopping; the V-Class swallows a whole family's bags. The car waits at the outlet, so you can drop bags off during the day.

FAQ

How many shops are there?

More than 150 brand stores, premium and luxury brands: the current list is on the outlet's own website.

How long should we plan at the outlet?

4 to 5 hours is enough for most guests; if you want more, the day is yours.

Can we go on a Sunday?

The shops are closed on Sundays: we recommend Monday to Saturday.

Are the big fashion houses in Budapest or Vienna?

Some are in both cities; several of the biggest names have a boutique in Vienna but not in Budapest: Bulgari, Van Cleef & Arpels, Dior, Cartier and Hermès among them, as well as the official Patek Philippe retailers. Tell us which houses you are looking for and we plan the day around them: your car waits while you shop.

Can you pick us up at Vienna Airport?

Yes: Vienna-Schwechat is about half an hour from the outlet. It can be combined with your arrival day, ask for a quote.

How many of us can travel?

E-Class and S-Class: up to 3 passengers; V-Class: up to 5 passengers with luggage.
