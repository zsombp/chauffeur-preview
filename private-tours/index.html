The Royal Palace · 4 hours

Gödöllő

Sisi's favourite home, 20 minutes north-east of the city.

  • 4 hours€300

Fixed price. Tax & tolls included.

The Royal Palace of Gödöllő was the summer residence of Emperor Franz Joseph and Queen Elisabeth, "Sisi". Walk the royal apartments, the baroque theatre and the park, and hear how the imperial couple actually lived here: hidden doors, private rooms, everyday life at the court of the Austro-Hungarian Monarchy. Your chauffeur waits at the gate; the palace is yours for as long as you like within the 4 hours.

Full day at the lake · 8 hours

Lake Balaton & Herend

Hungary's "sea", a 1,000-year-old abbey and the home of Herend porcelain.

  • 8 hours€650

Fixed price. Tax & tolls included.

Start at Herend, where porcelain has been hand-painted since 1826: see the manufactory and museum. Continue to the Tihany peninsula: the Benedictine Abbey above the lake, lavender fields and one of the finest views in Hungary. Finish in Balatonfüred, the country's first official resort, for a walk along the lakeside promenade. Around 8 hours door to door, with a lunch stop by the water.

  • Herend Porcelain Manufactory
  • Tihany Abbey & peninsula
  • Balatonfüred promenade
