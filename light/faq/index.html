Before your journey

Find the essentials here. For arrangements specific to your journey, contact our team before booking.

How do I request a booking?

Choose a service and vehicle class, then send your travel and contact details. The request is not a confirmed booking.

Are prices fixed?

The service uses fixed prices with tax and tolls included. Where no price is listed, request a quote. Hourly services show a rate and minimum duration.

Which Mercedes classes are available?

E-Class, V-Class and S-Class are shown across the service pages. You can also enquire about G-Class and VIP V-Class.

Should I book S-Class in advance?

Yes. S-Class has limited availability. Ask about a VIP V-Class or G-Class alternative.

Where is the Budapest Airport meeting point?

Your chauffeur meets you in the arrival hall with a name sign.

Do you track arriving flights?

Budapest Airport transfers include flight tracking. Add your arriving flight number to the request.

What is the airport waiting policy?

Ask our team to confirm waiting arrangements and flight-delay handling before booking.

What is the minimum hourly booking?

E-Class, V-Class and S-Class hourly service has a minimum duration of 3 hours. V-Class private tours have a minimum duration of 4 hours.

How much luggage can I bring?

Provide your bag count and sizes. Our team needs these details to check the vehicle fit.

Can I request a child seat or accessibility arrangements?

Describe your requirements when enquiring. Confirm suitable arrangements with our team before booking.

How can I pay?

Ask our team to confirm payment methods and timing before booking.

How do I change or cancel a booking?

Contact our team with your booking details. Ask for the applicable cancellation and amendment terms before confirming your journey.

What is provided on board?

Leather interior, climate control, free Wi-Fi, bottled water and onboard chargers.

Can I arrange travel late at night?

The service offers 24/7 availability. Include your required date and pickup time when requesting a booking.
